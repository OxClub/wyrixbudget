# Play Store materials (drafts: edit before use)

## Listing
**Title (30 max):** Wyrix: Budget & Money Planner
**Short description (80 max):** Private budgets, bills, goals and forecasts. Works offline. No account.

**Full description**
Wyrix is a private money manager that keeps everything on your phone. No sign-up, no ads, no internet needed.

Track income, expenses and transfers across cash, bank accounts, cards and wallets.
Set budgets by month, week or year, split them by category and get alerts before you overspend.
Save toward goals, see what to put aside each day, week or month.
Never miss a bill or renewal: reminders, mark-as-paid, and a clear view of what your subscriptions really cost.
Track loans and cards, see how much of each payment is interest, and compare payoff strategies.
Forecast where your balance is heading from the bills and income you have recorded.
Snap receipts (encrypted on your device) and let on-device text recognition suggest the details.
Export reports as PDF, Excel or CSV. Back up with an optional password.
Built with India in mind: ₹ with lakh grouping, April financial year, UPI/NEFT/RTGS/IMPS, GST tagging and an income-tax estimator. Supports other currencies with exchange rates you control.
Lock the app with a PIN or fingerprint. Hide amounts when others are looking.

Estimates, forecasts and calculators are for planning only and are not financial, tax or legal advice.
